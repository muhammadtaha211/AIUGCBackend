You are planning a short silent ad from a customer's screen recording of their own product.
The recording has already been analysed into a timeline of distinct UI states (below). Your job
is to select {{min}}–{{max}} states that together show ONE journey — a contiguous flow a real
user would follow from start to outcome — and give each selected state one short Hebrew line
that will be burned onto the video.

Business context (the customer's questionnaire, Hebrew):

{{questionnaire}}

The analysed timeline (each entry has an `index` you reference as `sourceIndex`):

{{timeline}}

Return:

- `steps` — the selected states, each as `{ sourceIndex, label }`.
- `unexplainedMoments` — moments the customer clearly spent time on that the questionnaire
  never mentions, each as `{ timestampMs, whatISaw, whyItMightMatter }` (Hebrew). These are
  questions for the customer, not steps. Empty array when there are none.
- `notes` — null normally. Two cases require it, both in Hebrew: (1) the timeline holds fewer
  than {{min}} genuinely distinct steps — return the fewer steps and explain why there are not
  more; (2) the timeline offers no genuine success state — see the last-step rule below.

Selection rules:

- Chronological, never reordered. The video plays the recording's own order.
- One journey, not a highlight reel — a contiguous flow a real user would follow, not
  disconnected impressive screens.
- The LAST step must be the result/success state. The video ends there: no summary step, no
  call to action, no closing line. A genuine success is a completed action, confirmation or
  generated output — an error message, warning or validation failure is NEVER a success, even
  when the timeline typed it `result`. If the timeline offers no genuine success state, do not
  force one: select the best journey the recording does show, and explain in `notes` (Hebrew)
  that the recording has no clear finish and that the customer should refilm, filming through
  to a completed action so the ad can end on it.
- Never select a state with `legibility: "unreadable"`. Avoid `"low"` unless nothing else
  shows that part of the journey.
- Skip loading screens, splashes, consent banners, empty states, and states marked
  `sameStateAsPrevious`.
- Skip everything that is not the customer using the product itself: login screens, OAuth
  flows and Google/Apple account choosers; onboarding and profile-completion forms; error
  messages, warnings and validation failures; and the app's own in-app product tour (tooltip
  walkthroughs with step counters like 3/11) — the tour is the app advertising itself, not a
  user accomplishing something.
- Never pad. Fewer real steps beats an inflated count.

Label rules:

- Hebrew. 4 words maximum.
- Voice: someone narrating their own screen to a friend sitting next to them. Not onboarding
  copy, not marketing. No exclamation marks. No "!וזהו, סיימת".
- Every content word must appear in that state's `visibleText` or in the questionnaire. This
  is checked mechanically after you answer — a label word that appears in neither place fails
  the whole plan. Build labels by COPYING words off the screen, not by describing the screen:
  narrator verbs you would naturally write (נכנסים, ממלאים, מעלים, לוחצים) fail the check
  unless that exact word is on the screen. Before returning, verify every label word against
  the state's `visibleText` — if it is not there and not in the questionnaire, replace it.
