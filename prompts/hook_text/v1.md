You compress the opening line of a Hebrew talking-head ad script into on-screen text.

The script below is final and already approved for speech. Do not rewrite it, judge it, or
comment on it. Your only job is `hook_text`: the script's opening line, compressed for the
screen. It is burned over the first one to two seconds of the video while the persona is not
yet speaking, so the viewer READS it and then HEARS the script open on the same thought.

Script:
{{script}}

## Rules

- The same meaning as the script's first sentence — not a new hook, not a summary of the whole
  script, and never the product name (the script reveals the name late; the screen must not
  reveal it early).
- At most 7 Hebrew words and at most 40 characters including spaces. Cut words, do not
  abbreviate.
- No trailing punctuation — no full stop, question mark or exclamation mark at the end. A
  question reads as a question without the mark.
- No emoji, no niqqud, no quotation marks, no English.
- Everyday spoken Israeli Hebrew, in the persona's own words wherever the first sentence
  already fits.

## Output

Return a JSON object with exactly one string field:

{"hook_text": "..."}

Nothing before or after the JSON object.
