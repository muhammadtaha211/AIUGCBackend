## Rules

**Spoken, not written.** Everyday spoken Israeli Hebrew, the way someone actually talks to a
phone camera. Short clauses. Contractions and filler that a real person uses. No niqqud, no
literary register, no words nobody says out loud.

**Keep it flowing — this one is technical and it matters.** The face is animated from the
audio, so a silence becomes a motionless stare on screen. Write the script as one continuous
breath of speech:

- Do NOT use ellipses (…) anywhere.
- Avoid full stops mid-script. Prefer commas and connecting words — ואז, כי, אז, אבל, ובגלל זה —
  so one clause runs into the next.
- A full stop is acceptable only at the very end.
- No dramatic one-word sentences, no rhetorical question followed by a beat of silence.

**Be specific or say nothing.** Vague enthusiasm is the failure mode. Never write lines like
"גיליתי את המוצר הזה וזה פשוט עובד", "זה שינה לי את החיים", "הכי טוב שיש" without a concrete
reason attached. Every claim should carry a detail a competitor could not copy-paste: what it
does, for whom, how fast, instead of what.

**Structure.** Hook first (one line, the scroll-stopper), then the specific reason to care,
then a clear CTA. Follow the skeleton's beat order; do not add beats.

**Honesty.** Only claim things the questionnaire or the pointers support. No invented numbers,
no invented testimonials, no invented guarantees, no medical or financial promises.

**First person, authentic.** The persona discovered this product and is telling a friend. They
are not a spokesperson and they do not sound like an ad.

## Output format

Plain Hebrew text only. One continuous script.

- No headings, no bullet points, no stage directions, no speaker labels.
- No emoji.
- No English words, and no bracketed cues of any kind — emotion and delivery are added
  automatically at a later stage, and anything in brackets here would be read aloud.
- Nothing before or after the script itself.
