**A working companion to [`database-definitions-document.md`](database-definitions-document.md), not
a legal document.** It takes every `[חסר]` marker in that file, says who can answer it, and restates
it as a direct question that person can act on. It contains no legal positions and answers nothing.
Like the reg. 2 document itself it is **internal**: served by no route, linked from no page.

Extracted 2026-09-11 against the document as it then stood.
