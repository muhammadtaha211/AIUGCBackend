## The rulings

- **The overlay route is dead.** Seedance regenerates the audio, so the mouth is always slightly
  off, and slightly off is enough.
- **The re-sync route is dead.** A lip-sync pass over a generated clip is not the way.
- **Kling Avatar v2 Pro is rejected** on its result.
- **Voiceover over unrelated footage is out** for anything but a physical product: narration only
  fits when she is showing the thing she talks about. Physical products stay parked
  ([[0017-service-b-single-setting-selfie-talking-head]]).
- **For generated clips, her look, the props and the scene are set in the reference image,** not
  left to the video prompt.
- **A גוש טקסט background clip should be a pattern interrupt:** something odd enough that the viewer
  keeps watching to see what she is doing.
- **Locked decision #12 stays locked.** Nothing in these tests argues for reopening it.
