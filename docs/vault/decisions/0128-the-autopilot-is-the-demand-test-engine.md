# 0128 — The autopilot is the founder's demand-test engine for candidate consumer apps; Tavnit stays as it is

Tags as in [[0127-the-instagram-autopilot]]: **FOUNDER-REPORTED** (his ruling or reason),
**REASONED**, **PLANNING DEFAULT** (planning's, not ruled; it stands unless he strikes it),
**MEASURED**. 0127 built the autopilot to post Tavnit's own ads; this note gives it a second job and
says what is not done. **Options offered:** the task prompt records only what he ruled out
(decision 4, and the sliver in 3); no other alternative was relayed.

## The decisions (FOUNDER-REPORTED, 2026-10-28, not up for relitigation)

1. **Tavnit stays as it is.** Nothing more is built for customers until there is data; the
   autopilot keeps running. His reason: about 6k views across all accounts and videos produced
   three signups, so more investment waits for traction. (The per-video figures are in
   state-of-play, FOUNDER-REPORTED, not repo-verified.)
2. **The autopilot is his demand-test engine for candidate consumer apps.** Per candidate: a new
   Instagram account, a clickable prototype (not an MVP, never launched during the test), one demo
   recording, Service A variants across hooks and sounds, and a waitlist page linked from the bio.
   Measured per account: total signups and signups per view. His reasons: the feed's viewers are
   consumers, so consumer apps are what it can test; the marginal cost is near zero (the box is
   paid for, a demo is analysed once per upload, hook lines cost cents); only what gets traction
   gets built.
   - **His citation for the analysis cost is the falsified claim.** He cited
     `every-upload-spends-2.40`; that note is [[every-upload-spends-2.40]] (superseded). The live
     figures are [[analyze-demo-cost]]: ₪1.13–2.72 an analysis, mean ₪1.82, over 14 analyses made
     in one attempt, read 2026-10-02 on 4.6, before the switch to 5.5. They are not re-measured on
     5.5 and are older than 14 days at this writing, so re-measure before quoting one
     (CONVENTIONS). The reason stands on either: an analysis is a few shekels. The hook lines' cost is 0127's own estimate,
     about ₪0.13 a Claude call (REASONED).
3. **The hook on his own posts names the widest audience the demo pays off for, never a sliver.**
   Structures from a competitor's viral posts may be borrowed; figures are only his own, never
   invented (the reasoning of [[0091-text-block-texts-ten-skeletons-truth-rules-free-edit]]
   decision 10, §2 there and its amendment's item 10: a figure the business itself supplied may be
   used, an invented one never).
   - **His reasons**, from his observation of that competitor (FOUNDER-REPORTED, not verified
     here): its viral hooks named «creators»; its founder-specific hooks capped at 5–10k views;
     a new account of theirs flopped.
   - **Planning's reason (REASONED):** a sliver of the test batch skips and the platform never
     extends it, and a broad hook over a demo that doesn't pay off loses completion.
   - **This resolves [[0061-landing-positioning-two-services-no-results-claims]] §5's deferred ICP
     question for his marketing:** Tavnit's own posts address anyone with something showable who
     posts to sell, not app founders. A dated pointer sits on 0061 §5. This record changes no
     landing copy, and no generator or prompt: the hook lines the autopilot writes are still the
     wizard's own, on the business's questionnaire (0127, "Hook lines").
4. **Not doing:** a pivot by reverse-engineering or cloning another product; marketing anyone
   else's product; analytics in the product for now (he reads Instagram's insights by hand, per
   account). His reasons: distribution, not code, is what a clone can't copy; his energy goes to
   products of his own; reading by hand suffices for a few accounts.

## PLANNING DEFAULT (planning's, not ruled; it stands unless he strikes it)

**The build threshold.** A candidate is built when its waitlist signups reach 500 ÷ its monthly
price in shekels ÷ the waitlist-to-paid rate he assumes, within 14 days of its first post. His
stated bar is ₪500 a month (FOUNDER-REPORTED). The arithmetic: payers needed = 500 ÷ price, signups
needed = payers ÷ rate. ILLUSTRATION only, neither number his: ₪25 a month at an assumed 5% needs
500 ÷ 25 ÷ 0.05 = 400 signups.

**A REASONED check of it against his own reported numbers** (state-of-play; FOUNDER-REPORTED, not
repo-verified): about 100 views a video and three signups in about 6k views is about 0.05% of views
becoming a signup. A candidate account posting four a day (0127 §9's count for a one-character
page) for 14 days gets about 5,600 views, which is about three signups at that rate. The threshold
is 100 signups even at ₪50 a month and a 10% rate (500 ÷ 50 ÷ 0.10). So it is met only if a
candidate's waitlist converts views far better than Tavnit's landing does, or its reach is far
above 100 a video; a miss at 14 days may read low reach rather than low demand. A reach floor
before a miss counts as «no» is his to set.
