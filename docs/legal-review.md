# Legal review — `/terms` and `/privacy`

`apps/web/src/app/(legal)/terms/page.tsx` and `apps/web/src/app/(legal)/privacy/page.tsx` are the
service's Hebrew terms of use and privacy policy. They are static server components; the shared
building blocks live in `apps/web/src/app/(legal)/legal.tsx`.

**Every factual statement in both pages was verified against the repo on 2026-09-02** — retention
periods, the vendor list and regions, cookie and storage key names, what the pipeline does, the
automatic technical checks in terms §7, and the credit and quota mechanics in terms §8. Those
statements are not open questions; they describe code that exists.

**On 2026-09-03 the founder resolved every review marker.** Until then the pages carried visible
`[LAWYER: …]` notes flagging sentences whose legal position had not been decided. Each note is
recorded verbatim below with the position now taken, the statutory basis for it, and the one thing
counsel should still confirm. No marker remains in the code.

Counsel should read the two live pages together with this file. The tables are the review: the
pages state positions, this file states why. The decision itself is recorded in the project vault
at [`docs/vault/decisions/0014-legal-page-positions.md`](vault/decisions/0014-legal-page-positions.md),
with the two open problems it surfaced in `docs/vault/defects/`.

**How the statutes were checked.** The statutory texts behind the positions below were checked
against official Reshumot / gov.il publications where those could be fetched, and against two
independent mirrors of the same text where they could not — `nevo.co.il` and `he.wikisource.org`
were both unreachable during the check. Every source in the Sources section carries a grade saying
which of the two it is; three points rest on secondary sources only and are graded `[S]`. Nothing
below should be treated as a legal opinion — it is a founder's reading of the statutes, written
down so counsel can correct it quickly.

The operator is a sole trader registered as **עוסק פטור** (VAT-exempt dealer). The service is
**B2B only**. Neither page shows the operator's ID number; the dealer number appears on receipts
and is given on request.
